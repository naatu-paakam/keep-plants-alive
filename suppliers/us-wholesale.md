# US Wholesale & Dropship Suppliers

Research notes for sourcing plant care tools. Organized by supplier type.

---

## Specialty Garden Irrigation & Hardware

### DripDepot (dripdepot.com)
- **Type:** US wholesaler / retailer, offers contractor pricing
- **Best for:** Drip stakes, emitters, tubing, fittings, timers, soaker hose
- **Notes:** Good API/data feeds; carries name-brand and generic; bulk pricing available. Already sells the 8" watering stakes we want.
- **Contact:** info@dripdepot.com
- **Reseller program:** Contact for wholesale account

### Orbit Irrigation (orbitonline.com)
- **Type:** Manufacturer (US brand, made in China)
- **Best for:** Timers, controllers, hose fittings, drip kits
- **Notes:** Available through their distributor network; also on Amazon. Look for their B2B / dealer program.

### Rain Bird (rainbird.com)
- **Type:** Manufacturer, professional-grade
- **Best for:** Controllers, emitters, timers — premium tier
- **Notes:** Primarily B2B/contractor but has retail line. Check distributor locator on site.

## Row Cover, Fabric & Protection

### Agribon / Fiberweb (via US distributors)
- **Type:** Manufacturer
- **Best for:** Floating row cover fabric (AG-19, AG-30, AG-50)
- **Notes:** Sold by the roll. Find distributors via Johnny's Selected Seeds, Greenhouse Megastore.

### Greenhouse Megastore (greenhousemegastore.com)
- **Type:** US wholesale / retail
- **Best for:** Row cover, shade cloth, cold frames, poly film, frost blankets
- **Notes:** Ships to individuals and businesses. Volume pricing. Great catalog for covers + structure items.
- **Wholesale:** Create a commercial account on site

### Gardener's Supply Company (gardeners.com)
- **Type:** Retail + curated catalog
- **Best for:** Pop-up cloches, season extenders, quality garden tools
- **Notes:** Not a supplier to resell from — use as benchmark for pricing and product validation

## Seed Starting & Propagation

### Bootstrap Farmer (bootstrapfarmer.com)
- **Type:** US manufacturer + direct seller
- **Best for:** Plug trays, fabric grow bags, humidity domes, flood tables
- **Notes:** Quality focused, ships from US. They sell direct but may have wholesale tiers. Good brand alignment.
- **Contact:** Contact form on site for bulk pricing

### Vivosun (vivosun.com)
- **Type:** Brand (ships from US warehouses, sourced from China)
- **Best for:** Heat mats, grow lights, grow tents
- **Notes:** Already on Amazon. Wholesale inquiries via their site.

---

## Sensors & Monitoring

### XLUX / Generic (Amazon / AliExpress OEM)
- **Type:** Chinese OEM, sold under multiple brand names
- **Best for:** Analog soil moisture meters
- **Notes:** $3–8 landed cost from AliExpress. Can private-label. Minimum orders typically 100–500 pcs.

### Xiaomi Mi Flora / VegTrug
- **Type:** Retail brand
- **Best for:** Bluetooth plant sensors
- **Notes:** Buy from US distributors or direct from AliExpress. Not easily private-labelable at small MOQ.

## China OEM / Alibaba (Private Label Candidates)

These categories have the best private-label margin potential once volume is validated:

| Product | Search Term on Alibaba | Typical MOQ | Est. Unit Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ceramic watering stakes | "terracotta watering spike" | 100–500 | $0.80–$2.50 |
| Pop-up garden cloches | "garden cloche cover popup" | 50–200 | $2–$6 |
| Frost blanket (nonwoven) | "plant frost protection cover fleece" | 50 pcs | $1–$3 |
| Soil moisture meter | "soil moisture meter 3 in 1" | 100 | $1.50–$3 |
| Drip emitter kits | "drip irrigation kit garden" | 50 sets | $3–$10 |
| Fabric grow bags | "fabric plant grow bags" | 100 | $0.50–$1.50 |

**Recommended Alibaba sourcing flow:**
1. Search product, filter by "Verified Supplier" + "Trade Assurance"
2. Request sample (usually $10–30 shipped)
3. Validate quality, then negotiate MOQ
4. Use Flexport or Freightos for freight quotes once ordering > 50kg
